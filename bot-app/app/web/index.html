Questions

Is my money safe?
Yes. J81 connects through Deriv's official API with trade-only permission — it can open and close trades on your behalf, but it can never withdraw or move your funds. You can revoke access in your Deriv settings at any time.
Do I need to know how to trade?
No. J81 suggests calls for you and can trade automatically to a profit target. You can also start on a practice account with virtual money to learn first.
What does it cost?
J81 is free to use. A small markup of up to 3% of a winning contract's payout (Deriv's standard developer markup) may apply on trades — never more than that, and never on your stake.
Can I lose money?
Yes — trading carries risk and you can lose your stake. That's why J81 offers a practice mode, take-profit and stop-loss limits, and starts everyone in demo. Never trade money you can't afford to lose.
Do I need a Deriv account?
Yes. If you don't have one yet, you can open a free Deriv account in a couple of minutes — there's a button on the connect screen.
