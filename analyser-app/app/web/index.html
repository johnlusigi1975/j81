Indicators & expressions phase 2.2 · the math layer

Try a rule against your cached candles before backtesting it. Indicators compute on the fly; the expression is evaluated in a safe sandbox (no eval() — LLM-derived strings can't escape).

Names available: open close high low price · last_digit · your indicator ref (rsi). For multi-line indicators: macd_signal, bb_upper, stoch_k, etc.

Backtest results phase 2.3 · the truth filter

Replays each stored strategy against cached Deriv candles. Strategies that survive (win-rate ≥ 55%, positive P&L, enough trades) become eligible for decisions.

Decisions phase 2.3 · the brain's output

The Analyser reads current market state + all surviving strategies and emits a single decision (or honest "no trade"). It will not force a trade just to have an opinion.
